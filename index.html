Snakes & Ladders
Mission Shackleton

Race from Earth orbit to the permanently shadowed crater at the Moon’s south pole. Climb the rocket ladders, dodge the cosmic snakes.

How to play

  1. Place your tokens on square 1 and roll the die in turn.
  2. Move forward by the number rolled.
  3. Land at the bottom of a ladder: climb to the top.
  4. Land on a snake’s head: slide down to its tail.
  5. First to reach Shackleton on square 100 wins.

Key

Rocket ladder: go up
Cosmic snake: go down
🚀Start: square 1
🌑Finish: square 100

About Shackleton crater

A crater near the Moon’s south pole, about 21 km wide. Its floor never sees sunlight, and orbiters have found hints of water ice there. That makes it a prime target for future lunar missions.

Print on A4 portrait at 100% scale with “Background graphics” turned on. You need one die and a token per player.
